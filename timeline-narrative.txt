TIMELINE NEWSPAPER ARCHIVE: JUSTICE FOR BARRAN
A Story of Systemic Failure, Ghosting, and Ongoing Struggle
2021 - 2026

=== FEBRUARY 14, 2021: THE ATTACK ===

HEADLINE: "14-Year-Old Indigenous Boy Stabbed Three Times - Family Seeks Help"

On the morning of February 14, 2021, a 14-year-old Indigenous boy was attacked and stabbed three times. The attacker struck his left lung, his chest, and his back. The boy's iPhone in his front pocket stopped two additional stab attempts. He was rushed to Montreal Children's Hospital where he spent six days hospitalized with a chest tube for three days. Medical staff held him, comforted him, and saved his life. Despite living with disability and Type 2 diabetes, the boy showed incredible resilience. His first words leaving the hospital were: "I miss my siblings." Family support sustained him then. Family support sustains him now.

=== 2021-2023: THE DPJ INVOLVEMENT ===

HEADLINE: "Child Protection Services Involved But Withdrawn - Family Left Without Support"

Following the stabbing, the family reached out to DPJ (Direction de la Protection de la Jeunesse) for help. The agency became involved but provided inadequate support. By January 2023, DPJ was withdrawn from the case entirely - no adequate follow-up, education lost, mental health destroyed. Jordan's Principle, which guarantees no-delay services for First Nations children, was ignored. The family was left without support from the very institution that should have protected them.

=== NOVEMBER 19, 2023: CEASE-AND-DESIST ===

HEADLINE: "MNA Sends Cease-and-Desist Warning to Constituent Seeking Help"

When the father asked his MNA, Céline Haytayan (CAQ, Laval-des-Rapides), for help on DPJ and IVAC files, instead of assistance, her office sent a cease-and-desist warning. The warning stated: stop all contact (in-person, phone, email, social media) or face criminal harassment complaint and police involvement. This is what happened when a constituent asked for help from their elected representative.

=== 2021-2026: THE GHOSTING BEGINS ===

HEADLINE: "Assembly of First Nations Refuses to Help Indigenous Family"

The family reached out to the Assembly of First Nations (AFN) for support. AFN refused to help, stating that the family was not "status" or part of their band, despite being First Nations children in need of support. For over five years, AFN has remained silent.

HEADLINE: "Prime Minister's Office Ignores Pleas for Federal Intervention"

The family escalated to the Prime Minister's Office. No response. No action. Complete ghosting despite repeated contact attempts. The federal government has shown indifference to the systemic failures affecting Indigenous children.

HEADLINE: "Amnesty Canada Silent on Human Rights Violations"

Despite having a mandate to defend human rights, Amnesty Canada provided no response to requests for support. A human rights organization failed to advocate for vulnerable children.

HEADLINE: "Legal Profession Abandons Vulnerable Family"

Not a single lawyer stepped up to help protect vulnerable Indigenous children. The legal profession, bound by oath to uphold justice and defend the vulnerable, has broken that oath. Many lawyers are paid by the government, creating a conflict of interest that prevents them from challenging systemic failures.

HEADLINE: "Legal Aid Quebec Refuses Legal Support"

Legal Aid Quebec provided no response to requests for legal support. Despite a mandate to provide legal assistance to those in need, they refused to help a vulnerable family.

=== FEBRUARY 27, 2026: THE WELLNESS CHECK FAILURE ===

HEADLINE: "Governor General's Office Wellness Check Fails to Assess Children"

On February 27, 2026, the Governor General's office conducted a wellness check. The biological father only spoke with a receptionist - not with the children who needed help. No proper assessment of the children's needs. No real intervention. Just another institutional failure.

=== 2021-2026: FIVE YEARS OF SYSTEMIC FAILURE ===

HEADLINE: "Five Years of Ghosting Reveals Systemic Failure Across All Sectors"

From 2021 to 2026, this family has been ghosted by:
- Multiple elected officials (federal, provincial, municipal)
- Indigenous organizations (AFN)
- Human rights groups (Amnesty Canada)
- Government agencies (PMO, Legal Aid Quebec)
- The entire legal profession
- Child protection services (DPJ)

This is not an isolated case. This is systemic failure across all sectors of Canadian society. When politicians, Indigenous organizations, human rights groups, legal aid services, and the entire legal profession all fail to support vulnerable Indigenous children, it reveals institutional abandonment.

=== THE MOVEMENT: JUSTICE FOR ALL ===

HEADLINE: "Breaking the Cycle of Systemic Failure - A Movement for All Indigenous Children"

This movement is not just about Barran. It's about breaking the cycle of systemic failure that affects all Indigenous children in Canada. Every child deserves protection, support, and justice. The 2008 apology for residential schools promised change. But in 2026, the same systems still fail Indigenous children. Enough is enough. Truth and Reconciliation was not a conclusion - it was a beginning that was ignored.

=== CALL FOR ACCOUNTABILITY ===

HEADLINE: "Public Accountability Demanded - Officials Must Answer for Inaction"

Public accountability is the first step toward real change. When officials and organizations are held accountable for their inaction, systemic failures can begin to be addressed. Silence enables abuse. Speaking out demands justice. The reckoning is coming.
