## Who We Are - Why We Fight for Justice

### ROOTS IN OUTAOUAIS: A LEGACY OF SERVICE

I am Robert McGovern. My father was connected to Union Nationale - a man who believed in public service and standing up for what was right. He taught me that when you see injustice, you speak up. When you see vulnerable people being harmed, you act. That legacy runs through my family's veins.

Our family is rooted in the Outaouais region. We are part of this community. We have built lives here, raised children here, and contributed to this region for generations. Our roots run deep - in the soil, in the culture, in the institutions we've helped build.

### JOE McGOVERN: OUR FOUNDATION (1900s-1984)

Joe McGovern was a dedicated worker in the hospitality industry throughout the Outaouais region: Chez Henry (Gatineau), Standish Hall Hotel (Montcalm Street, Hull), and La Chaudiere Club (Aylmer, 1954). Joe was more than a worker - he was a community member who understood the importance of hospitality, service, and caring for others. He worked alongside lifelong friends like Gerry Barber and Dryden, building a legacy of hard work and community service. Joe McGovern passed away on May 27, 1984 - a date that changed our family forever.

### TERRY JAMES McGOVERN: GUARDIAN, HEALER, PIONEER (1967-present)

When Joe McGovern passed away, Terry James McGovern became my legal guardian at age 17 - just 9 months before my 18th birthday. At that young age, Terry stepped into a role that would define his life and inspire our family's healing mission. Terry took custody of me and my sister Cindy and worked together at his ranch, providing stability, care, and healing. Terry pioneered equestrian therapy work with vulnerable children at Ferme Terry James (977 Montee McLaren) - a ranch-based healing centre. He demonstrated that healing through animals, nature, and community care is possible. His vision of ranch-based healing became the foundation for the McGovern Arts Ranch Healing Centre.

### THE McGOVERN ARTS RANCH HEALING CENTRE VISION

Based on Terry James's pioneering work and Joe McGovern's commitment to community service, our family envisions a McGovern Arts Ranch Healing Centre that will provide healing for vulnerable children and families using equestrian therapy, art, cultural healing, and community care. Implementation will be slow, sustainable growth with a professional team, proper law firms, and accountants. Why this matters: Terry James showed us that healing is possible. Joe McGovern showed us that community service is a family value. We are fighting for justice so that Barran, Paisley, and my autistic boys can access the healing that Terry pioneered.
